☷ Before You Talk to a Doctor

4 quick steps to make your consultation efficient and clear.

  • ✓ Keep health records ready. Upload recent lab reports, scans, or discharge summaries.
  • ✓ Describe symptoms clearly: Note onset time, pain severity, and triggers.
  • ✓ List current medications & allergies. Keep active pill strips with you.
  • ✓ Test audio & camera before consultation for a smooth session.
