Everyday Precautions
Step-by-step health guidance
01

Maintain Hand Hygiene

Wash your hands regularly.

+
Wash your hands with soap and clean running water, especially before eating and after using the restroom.
02

Keep Your Surroundings Clean

Reduce germs around your home.

+
Keep frequently touched surfaces clean and dispose of household waste properly.
03

Drink Safe Water

Protect yourself from water-borne illness.

+
Drink water from a safe source. If the quality is uncertain, use an appropriate purification method.
04

Follow Medication Instructions

Take medicines as prescribed.

+
Follow the dose and timing given by your healthcare professional and never share prescription medicines.
05

Maintain a Balanced Diet

Support your overall health.

+
Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, proteins and adequate fluids in your daily diet.
06

Take Care During Seasonal Changes

Protect against common seasonal problems.

+
Stay hydrated, dress appropriately for the weather and take reasonable precautions during seasonal changes.

🟠 When Should You Contact a Healthcare Professional?
If you experience any of the following symptoms, please seek prompt medical attention:
  • Persistent or worsening symptoms over 48 hours
  • Difficulty breathing or sudden shortness of breath
  • Severe, sharp, unexplained pain
  • High or persistent fever
  • Sudden changes in consciousness
  • Any unexpected symptoms causing significant concern
✚ Need Urgent Help?
Emergency Line 112

If you believe you're experiencing a medical emergency, seek immediate help from emergency services or the nearest hospital emergency room.
